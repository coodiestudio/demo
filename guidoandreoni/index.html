About

The result becomes poetry

With over 12 years of experience documenting love stories, I work with digital and analog formats, favouring 35mm but also using 120, to bring a variety of perspectives.

I consider myself a silent observer who captures the most authentic feelings: love and joy, emotion and imperfect moments. Great stories are a matter of small details that reveal a thousand words. A quick glance, a gesture, a smile or a stroke are all the nuances that warm up your day in the most intimate way.

Mine is not just a technique, but a philosophy of shooting. It has always been important to me that images are captured with a truthful approach, and can hold the mood and atmosphere of a certain time and place.
