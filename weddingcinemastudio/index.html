The studio

We grew, then chose to grow smaller

We started by filming our friends' weddings. No planners, no luxury venues, no styled productions. Just real people in love, and honestly, sometimes plastic cups and no flowers at all.

4weddings a year, at the start

Word spread naturally: first among friends of friends, then from couples who found our work online and connected with the storytelling.

30+weddings a year, at the peak

At that point we realised the pace was not sustainable if we wanted to preserve the quality and the care behind each film. So we made a conscious decision to change direction.

Wedding Cinema Studio now focuses on a limited number of weddings each year. Every film is personally edited and curated by us, ensuring a high level of attention and consistency, in line with our approach and values.
